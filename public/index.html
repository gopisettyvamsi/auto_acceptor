VamsifyAuto Acceptor

Know which rides are worth it. Accept the right ones automatically.

Every ride request gets a clear rating with fare, pickup and drop distance, fuel cost and what you keep. Let the app accept only the rides that meet your targets.

Download for Android
Android 7.0 or later · works with Rapido, Uber and Ola driver apps

What you get

Earnings chip

A worth-it rating on every ride request: earnings per km, fuel cost and your take-home, based on your vehicle.

Auto-accept

Set a minimum fare, maximum pickup distance and minimum earnings per km. Matching rides are accepted for you.

Ride history

See today's earnings and every accepted ride with its rating, so you know where your day went.

Profit and loss

Earnings minus fuel, expenses and fixed costs for today, the week or the month, broken down by vehicle and app.

Your rules

Targets per vehicle, stricter peak hours, a daily goal, and optional alerts and declining of low-paying rides.

Set up in four steps

  1. Download and install the APK. Allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser when asked.
  2. Open Vamsify, then choose your vehicle, fuel price and mileage in Settings.
  3. Tap Enable service on the home screen and turn on Vamsify in Accessibility. On Android 13 and later, if it is blocked, open App info, tap the menu and choose Allow restricted settings.
  4. Switch on Earnings chip, Auto-accept, or both, then go online in your driver app.
Automating actions in driver apps may be against their terms and could lead to account suspension. Use at your own risk. © Vamsify
