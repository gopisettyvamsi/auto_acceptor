Auto Acceptor

Automatically taps Accept on ride requests in Rapido, Uber or Ola driver apps when the offer matches your preferences.

Download APK
  1. Install the APK (allow "install unknown apps" for your browser).
  2. Open Auto Acceptor, set min fare, max pickup km and delay, switch ON, Save.
  3. Tap Enable in Accessibility settings and turn on Auto Acceptor. On Android 13+, if blocked: App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings.
  4. Go online in your driver app and keep the screen on.

Using automation may violate the driver apps' terms and can get your account suspended. Use at your own risk.
